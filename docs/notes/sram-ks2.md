# SRAM timing on the KS2

Measured with the SRAM tester (issue #90; `fpga/ks2/top_ks2_sramtest.v`, `tools/sramtest.py`) on 2026-11-07. The KS2's SRAM is an IS61WV102416FBLL-8 (8 ns), so these are a best case: the KS3's 10 ns part is to be measured again.

Each pin changes in 1.25 ns steps (OSERDESE2 at 800 Mb/s); DQ is sampled every 1.25 ns (ISERDESE2), 78 ps finer with the IDELAY taps. Step 0 is where a cycle's address leaves the FPGA; a capture at step c samples DQ at the moment step c leaves, as sram_ctl's input flip-flops do. Each sweep moves one setting, the others at the cycle's defaults, over 65536 words of random data a row.

## Reference: 35 ns (28 steps)

About the retro-spectrum build's 35.7 ns cycle, which has run for days without errors on the same pads: if this fails, suspect the rig, not the timing.

Defaults: /OE low 7-28, capture at step 28, /WE low 7-21, data driven 7-28 (after a read from 7). Steps are 1.25 ns from the address leaving the FPGA.

Loopback: driven from step 7, seen by the FPGA from step 10 (per DQ bit, D15..D0: 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10), so the pads' out-and-in delay is about 3 steps.
